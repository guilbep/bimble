## Every private IFC through import-ifc and pack (18 files, 4.1 GB; numbers only)
- Asked (Pierre): import the office's IFC files and the local ones, to .bimble and
  .bimbleview, look at the warnings and errors, propose fixes.
- Ran: `import-ifc`, `pack`, then `info` on both outputs, for each file. 17 of 18 import and
  pack; the 18th is a Google Drive file not downloaded on this machine (read timed out): not
  Bimble. Two files with few elements (46 and 12) are lift models: every product is imported.
- Found and fixed:
  - `bimble info` (and views, sheets, schedules, project on the command line) failed on a
    .bimbleview ("invalid utf-8"): they read it as a .bimble. A .bimbleview is now treated
    like an IFC (views and the rest in its side file).
  - `pack` didn't say the geometry warnings (import did): it prints them now.
  - "N storeys placed offset or turned in plan" on 15 of 17 files: in each, every storey has
    exactly its building's frame (site coordinates, turned), the usual Revit export.
    Said now only for a storey placed differently from its building. Test added.
  - A curved brick wall drawn 4.0 m tall instead of 3.4 m (2.47 vs 2.10 m³; IfcOpenShell
    2.10260, ours now 2.102604): its two polygonal bounded half-spaces have a composite-curve
    boundary whose arcs and lines are hundreds of metres apart (a broken export). IfcOpenShell
    cuts by the plane alone when a boundary doesn't close (checked on synthetic cases: a 10 mm
    gap, a missing side, arcs and lines apart); Bimble closed it with a guessed line, or gave
    up. Now the same as IfcOpenShell; fixture and test (fails on the old code).
  - "no usable geometry" now says why: 2 doors with only a 2D footprint, slabs and walls with
    no shape at all in the file. IfcOpenShell draws none of them either.
  - The same IFC imported twice gave two models with different hashes: the project got a new
    random id each time. It keeps the IfcProject's GlobalId now (test).
  - Openings not cut when an IFC is opened or packed (`pack` now says it): ~380 in one file,
    ~110 in another. The host slabs are Breps with corners written twice 0.03–0.07 mm apart,
    not closed for the kernel. When the kernel refuses a solid, it is sewn (points within
    0.1 mm welded) and tried once more; valid solids never take that path. One file: 387 → 4
    openings not cut; another 108 → 95. Pack time on the largest with openings 1.8 → 2.4 s
    (the cuts now happen).
  - In a .bimble, the same slabs' openings failed silently (mesh-time cut): fixed by the
    same sewing; most of those openings were also holes in the Brep already (volumes changed
    by ≤ 0.02 m³ per slab). ADR 0010 amended (sewing, unclosed boundaries).
  - Final run: element counts unchanged on all 17 files, both outputs reopen, a second import
    is byte-identical, import and pack times unchanged on the largest files.
  - Left: 2 slabs that are open surface models (IfcFaceBasedSurfaceModel, faces not oriented
    the same way) and 1 Brep with faces turned inside out: they need faces reoriented.
- Kept: "no IfcBuildingStorey" (one file has none: elements go to a synthetic storey).
- Proposed, not done: plans and sections in the building's frame (project north). These
  models' buildings are turned in site coordinates; Bimble draws world axes, so plans
  are turned and "north" sections cut across the grid.
