# 27 Nov 2026 — ticket VIS1: draw only what can be seen

Times are the commits' (`git log cc/vis1`), Central European Time. The numbers are those of ADR 0078
amendment 7, measured by `ccore/tools/drive.mjs` on the machine's GPU (Apple M1 Pro, Mesa, ANGLE on Vulkan,
headless Chromium 153, then 158 from 13:10); the owner's own project is described only as "a local folder of 17 IFC
files, 110.5 M triangles".

## What happened

- **Pierre's reports** (preview.6, his 17-link project): "moving: none", 4.3-74 M triangles a still frame in
  1,136-6,233 draws, input → frame 400-730 ms, "proxies drawn 0", boxes while orbiting and a refill "one by one ...
  taking ages". He delegated the choices ("performance is everything", "do what games do"); the orchestrator set
  the priorities (P0-a to P0-c before the culling) and asked for a reusable investigation tool.
- **Causes found**: his stored `moving_fps` was 0 ("Draw everything": no moving frame at all); the panel counted the
  open model's proxies only (his links' proxies were drawn: 194 cells); the line-edge pass was 55-65 % of a frame's
  GPU time; draw calls cost about 5 ms at 6,000 (not the bottleneck); a pick read link triangles back from the GPU
  for every element (0.7-1.1 s); the budget's bisection over 46,800 batches made each fill slice 290 ms of script.
- **Built** (the commits of `cc/vis1`): `drive.mjs` (real gestures, frame and input logs, profiles,
  screenshots, an MCP face); moving follows input and "Draw everything" is kept under 100 ms; edges found on screen
  from an offscreen target; the id-buffer pick; the depth pyramid of the last picture and per-element culling with
  multi-draw (setting `draw_visible`: off by default, exact on the synthetic sweep, not yet on the owner's kind of project); every optimisation a setting; proxies instead of boxes while moving; the refill drawn offscreen; a
  bucketed budget cut; Chromium's short TMPDIR (its singleton socket's path was too long under a deep scratch folder).
- **Who decided**: Pierre delegated; the choices by Claude on ticket VIS1 with the orchestrator, recorded in ADR 0078
  amendment 7 with their alternatives.
