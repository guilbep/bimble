# 27 Nov 2026 — ticket VIS2: the page's hidden-surface skipping exact on a real project

Times are the commits' (`git log cc/vis2`), Central European Time. The owner's project is described only as "a local
folder of 17 linked IFC files, 110.5 M triangles"; its numbers went to the orchestrator, not here. Measured with
`ccore/tools/drive.mjs` on the machine's GPU (Apple M1 Pro, Mesa, ANGLE on Vulkan, headless Chromium 158).

## What happened

- **The box fault, measured first.** The truth test reproduced on the owner's project: 4,365 seen elements not drawn on
  a close camera, the picture 237,853 pixels off. A diagnosis hook (`bench.whyMissing`) named each missing element's
  reason; another (`bench.boxAudit`) rebuilt every element's box from its link's mesh asked again: the page's own-frame
  boxes were right (0 bad on three links of 21,192, 783 and 30,295 elements). The missing elements' boxes were at
  2.5e9 mm: the open model is empty, the links georeferenced (Swiss coordinates), f32 steps 256 mm there. Fixed by a
  render origin (`gl/origin.ts`): f64 on the CPU, relative coordinates on the GPU (WebGL2, then WebGPU).
- **A second fault behind it**: copies of links opened from their rows drew their link's first shape (instanced groups
  at offset 0). Found by reading back the GPU's instance buffers for a missing element (`getBufferSubData`).
- **Truth on the owner's project after both**: 0 missing, 0 pixels differ on 6 cameras (close, far, inside, twice each).
  `draw_visible` on by default (the core's default: the native app reads it too).
- **The orchestrator's interrupt**: `lab_open_url` failed on GitHub's runner (217.8 ms to the opening card). The card is
  now drawn by `index.html`'s inline script while the HTML is parsed; the test throttles the CPU 4x, 6x and 10x
  (10x failed on the old code: 294.7 ms). Cherry-picked to `next` at once.
- **"11 of 12" after fast turns**: a link's detail loading made the pyramid stale (`detailGen`); a load no longer does
  (it only adds to a picture whose pyramid counted that link's proxies as the farthest depth). The check became the
  invariant: reused unless made stale.
- **`lab_webgpu_links`** failed on `next`: bisected to SHELL1's merge; the far link gives its detail back when its proxies
  come late (by design): the test's expectation was stale. Cherry-picked.
- **The page never idle on the owner's project**: a still picture's fill drew 0-triangle slices forever (a rounding in
  the budget's never-empty rule), then, once fixed, slices of a few thousand triangles for a fixed script cost of 50 ms
  (46,800 batches a frame): its own rate collapsed to the minimum budget and the fill took minutes; and the slices spent
  their budget on items the visibility test leaves out. Fixed: the cut counts only what may be drawn, a slice gets at
  least a whole frame's rate times a moving frame's time.
- **28 Nov (after the machine's failed suspend at 21:09 and a reboot)**: `next` merged again; the never-idle fix committed
  alone (c257171) for preview.8; the A/B on the owner's project; preview.7's page and host on the same gestures never go
  idle after an orbit either (so not SHELL1's doing). The inputs over 200 ms each traced in a CPU profile: a synchronous
  pixel read for the status bar behind the GPU, components re-asking the host on every selection, pivots scanning every
  element, histograms rebuilt at each motion start, a canvas resize (the properties panel) leaving the pyramid stale and
  the pyramid's read-back waiting behind the GPU process. Fixed and measured (ADR 0078 amendment 9, decisions 71 to 73);
  the press after Escape and the press that starts an orbit stay over 200 ms: the orchestrator released preview.8 with
  85ca5de and the notes say so.
- **A memory bug on the way**: `cc_settings.shell_mode` one byte short ("priority" and its NUL).
- **The web session log** for the owner's runs with the native host (route, setting, page module, Settings row).
- **The tile path** (the ticket's step 2) was not built: the orchestrator agreed it becomes its own tickets, planned in
  ADR 0078 amendment 9 with the numbers measured here.
