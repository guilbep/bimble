## What happened
- **Plan preview**: sheets rasterize through resvg (system fonts), so the preview is the exported drawing.
  `bimble plan … plan.png --dpi 150`; the viewer's Plan window shows the sheet live (zoom, recomputed only
  when a setting changes). Real file #2: PNG in 0.38 s.
- **I4, formwork-style plans** (ADR 0018). Read the office's A2 plans (kept out of the repo) for their
  conventions, then built them as a *plan template* rather than code: title, level note, dimension unit,
  hidden classes, legend words, title-block columns, `{fields}`. French example in `docs/templates/`.
  - Seen below the cut: tops filled lighter, painted from low to high (hidden-line removal by painting).
  - IfcGrid axes from IFC, including IfcIndexedPolyCurve; real file #1 has its grid repeated on 12
    storeys (192 axes → 16). Without grids, axes through the cut columns.
  - Dimension chains (axis to axis, then overall) in cm with architect's ticks: on real file #1 they read
    362 / 362 / 362 / 420.9 / 362 — the spacings on the office's own plan of that building.
  - Terrain (IfcGeographicElement) made the first real plan 104 m wide and too big for A0; now hidden by
    default with rebar, spaces and openings.
  - Sheet texts got anchors, rotation and bold, placed from Helvetica widths so SVG, PDF and PNG agree.
- **I2, history** (ADR 0017): `house.bimble-history/` with content-addressed objects (one per element,
  tree, commit), branches, HEAD; `bimble history init|commit|status|log|diff|branch|switch|merge`.
  Trying a merge on the demo (height changed on one branch, width on the other) showed element-level
  conflicts were too coarse and `--prefer` then dropped one change: merge became field by field.
- **I2, editing in the viewer** (ADR 0019): properties form (name, sizes, origin), move/copy/delete,
  placing columns, beams, walls and slabs by clicking on the storey level, undo/redo (Ctrl+Z/Y), save
  (Ctrl+S), History window (start, commit, branches, switch, merge, log). Checked on the virtual display:
  copy, a size drag, undo, placing a column and a beam. Typing into fields could not be driven there
  (xdotool keys reach shortcuts but not text fields); the form logic is unit-tested instead.
