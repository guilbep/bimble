## The logo and the icons (branch logo, merged to main)
- Asked (Pierre): two raster pictures of the logo made into SVG (a round mark, 210 x 211 px, and
  the word "bim / ble" on two lines, 472 x 472 px), built with a work system he gave: plan,
  search, build and check as separate stations, one ticket at a time, each with an executable
  check that passes before the next ticket starts. Then: "the goal of those svg is for bimble to
  have icons" on the macOS app, the web app, Windows, Linux and the documentation.
- Decided (Pierre, "go, with the window icon, replace by the svg we designed"): the mark's frame
  is a square that is exactly the disc. Desktop icons draw the disc at 80 % of the side. A second
  wordmark with cream letters for dark pages. The running window gets the icon too.
- Done, each checked before the next:
  - No tracing tool is installed and the sources are small, so the letters were rebuilt as
    geometry (circles, arcs, straight stems) from measurements. The check renders the SVG with
    headless Chrome at the source's size and compares colour classes pixel by pixel outside a 1 px
    edge band. Its controls: the source itself passes, a blank fails, a picture with one 702 px
    part missing fails although 99.67 % of its pixels agree.
  - `packaging/logo.svg` (wordmark): 1 232 bytes, 40 path commands, the b defined once and used
    twice. Agreement 1.0000 on 212 053 px. Measured: stems 34 px wide, the b's bowl radius 46.6
    and counter radius 13, a 5 px slit between the b's stem and bowl, discs of radius 103.59
    (orange) and 104.93 (pink).
  - `packaging/logo-mark.svg` (mark): the wordmark's b and orange disc, moved 34 px up at scale
    1.00. Agreement 1.0000 on 41 184 px against the 210 x 211 source, then the frame made square
    (viewBox 207.18).
  - `scripts/make-icons.sh` regenerates `packaging/icon-32`, `-128`, `-256`, `-512` and `icon.png`
    (1024) with headless Chrome. Two runs give the same bytes. cargo-packager packs these PNGs
    into the macOS .icns and uses them for the Linux packages. For the Windows .msi it looks for
    an .ico among the icons (`find_ico`, cargo-packager 0.11.8) and there is none, so the
    installer has no icon, as before this work. They replace the earlier picture (ADR 0043
    amended).
  - Web page: `favicon.svg` and `favicon.png`, copied by `scripts/build-web.sh` from `packaging/`,
    two link lines in `web/index.html`. The build ran (1 min 9 s) and both files are in
    `web/dist`.
  - `packaging/logo-dark.svg`: the wordmark with cream letters (#F5F2EB), nothing else different.
    The README shows the wordmark at its top, the dark one on a dark theme.
  - Window icon: `window_icon()` in the viewer through eframe's own PNG decoding, no new
    dependency. A missing icon does not stop the viewer. Test `the_window_icon_is_the_orange_disc`.
