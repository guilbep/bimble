Third-party code and fonts in bimble-ccore (the program and its web/ folder)

Bimble itself is MIT (LICENSE). These parts come from others. Each line gives the name, the
licence as an SPDX identifier, and where its licence text is: the licences/ folder of this archive
when the text is in Bimble's repository, else the upstream file at the commit that was built.
